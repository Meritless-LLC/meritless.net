Frequently asked questions

Is this legal advice?

No. Our apps are for study and entertainment. They don’t create an attorney-client relationship, and they’re no substitute for a licensed lawyer who knows your situation.

I think an answer in Lazy Jurist is wrong.

You may be right — the law is messy, and some motions really are close calls. Email us with the card’s topic and wording, and tell us why. If you’re right, we’ll fix it and quietly pretend we always knew.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play without signing up for anything.

My progress disappeared.

Progress and settings are stored on your device. Deleting and reinstalling the app, or clearing your browser data for the web version, will reset them.

How do I get a refund for a purchase?

App Store purchases are processed by Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request one.

What data do you collect?

As little as possible. See our Privacy Policy for the details.
