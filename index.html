About the company

Findings of fact

  1. We make law apps. Meritless LLC develops and publishes law-themed games and study tools for iPhone, iPad, and the web.
  2. We are not a law firm. Nothing we make is legal advice, and using our apps doesn’t create an attorney-client relationship. Talk to a licensed lawyer about your actual case.
  3. The name is the joke. “Without merit” is what courts say on their way to tossing a claim. We took it as a challenge.
  4. We respect your privacy. Our apps are built to collect as little as possible. Read the privacy policy — it’s short, by legal standards.
