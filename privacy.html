Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026 · Applies to all Meritless LLC apps and websites

The short version: Our apps don’t ask for your name, don’t require an account, don’t sell your data, and don’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites. Game progress and settings stay on your device.

This policy explains how Meritless LLC (“Meritless,” “we,” “us”) handles information in connection with our mobile applications, including Lazy Jurist, and our websites, including meritless.net and lazyjurist.com (together, the “Services”). Unlike most of our footnotes, this page is meant to be taken seriously.

1. Information we collect

Information you don’t have to give us. Our apps do not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or other personal information in order to play.

Information stored on your device. Things like scores, streaks, settings, and progress are saved locally on your device so the app remembers where you left off. We don’t receive this information. Deleting the app deletes it.

Information you choose to send us. If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you put in your message. We use it only to respond to you and to improve our Services.

Information from Apple. If you download our apps from the App Store, Apple may share aggregated, anonymized information with us, such as download counts, crash reports, and usage statistics, if you’ve opted in to sharing with app developers in your device settings. This information does not identify you personally. Apple’s handling of your data is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Website server logs. Like nearly every website, our web hosts may automatically log technical information such as IP addresses, browser type, and pages requested, for security and to keep the site running. We don’t use this information to identify individual visitors.

2. What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell or rent personal information.
  • We don’t use third-party advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • We don’t track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.
  • We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children.

3. How we use information

We use the limited information described above to provide and operate the Services, respond to support requests, fix bugs, understand in aggregate how the Services are used, and comply with legal obligations.

4. Sharing

We don’t share personal information with third parties except: (a) with service providers that help us operate the Services (such as web hosting or email), who may use it only on our behalf; (b) when required by law, subpoena, or other legal process; or (c) in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case this policy will continue to apply to the information transferred.

5. Children

Our Services are intended for a general audience and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device remains under your control and is removed when you delete the app. Support emails are kept only as long as reasonably needed to help you and for our records. You may ask us to delete any personal information we hold about you by emailing us at the address below.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, California or the European Economic Area), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of your personal information. Because we collect so little, there usually isn’t much to request — but we’re happy to help. Email us and we’ll respond within a reasonable time.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the information we handle. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but collecting very little data is a pretty good start.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update it on this page and revise the effective date above. If an app adds a feature that changes how we handle data, we’ll update this policy before that feature ships.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@meritless.net.
